# Fondos de las secciones de MRB

Generados el 6 de octubre de 2026 con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Son imágenes decorativas de ambientes y objetos, sin representar oficinas reales de MRB ni clientes. Se conservó el fondo del inicio.

## Archivos finales

- `public/brand/hero-accounting.webp`: contabilidad e impuestos.
- `public/brand/hero-legal.webp`: constitución de sociedades, trámites y asesoría legal societaria.
- `public/brand/hero-payroll.webp`: Payroll y asesoría laboral.
- `public/brand/hero-audit.webp`: auditoría y consultoría.
- `public/brand/hero-contact.webp`: contacto.
- `public/brand/hero-office.webp`: inicio, servicios, nosotros y equipo.

## Especificación común de los prompts

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero background for MRB Business Consulting, accounting and advisory firm in Paraguay. Style: tasteful realistic commercial editorial photography. Composition: panoramic 16:9 horizontal, leave dark uncluttered negative space on left two thirds for website title, recognizable subject on right, shallow depth of field with soft defocused details. Palette: navy blue, slate, neutral wood, soft cool daylight, consistent modern office visual identity. Constraints: no people, no logos, no readable text, no watermark, no fake identifiable client data. Generate one image.

## Escenas de los prompts

- Accounting: Financial accounting desk with calculator, closed ledger, laptop with indistinct spreadsheet and neat financial report pages, office window reflections, no legible figures.
- Legal: Business registration and legal consultancy desk with neatly stacked folders, elegant fountain pen, signed-looking documents with entirely unreadable text, soft focus glass office background, no flags or official seals.
- Payroll: Human resources and payroll office desk with laptop showing indistinct schedule grid, notebook, organized employee folders and a navy meeting chair, no people or names visible.
- Audit: Financial audit workspace, close-up of neatly organized report pages, magnifying glass and calculator on modern desk, laptop in background, reports have abstract indistinct charts with no legible information.
- Contact: Welcoming modern business advisory office reception and meeting area, navy chairs, simple desk, phone and notebook, daylight through glass partitions, no company signage.

Las imágenes se comprimieron a WebP. El desenfoque y la capa azul son parte del diseño del sitio, para conservar el contraste del texto y adaptar el fondo a pantallas distintas.
